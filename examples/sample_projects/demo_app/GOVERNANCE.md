# Acme Demo — AI Governance

We maintain:

- A risk management system reviewed quarterly.
- A model card for each deployed model.
- Audit logs of every decision (record-keeping).
- Human oversight: every auto-rejection is reviewed by a recruiter.
- Adversarial testing before each release.
- Post-market monitoring with corrective action procedures.
- A Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA) on file.
