# Legal Disclaimer

The TRACE AI Act Risk Scanner is a **technical signal scanner**. It is **not legal advice** and does **not** certify compliance with Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (EU AI Act), Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) or any other law.

The scanner identifies patterns in code, configuration and documentation that **may indicate** alignment with categories defined in the AI Act and GDPR. The output is intended as **early-warning triage** for internal review by legal, technical and operational teams.

- A clean scan does not prove a system is compliant.
- A noisy scan does not prove a system is non-compliant.
- The scanner does not analyse model behaviour, training data or runtime decisions.
- The scanner does not infer intent.
